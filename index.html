How a hunt works

Four steps from your couch to your first capture.

1

Open the map

The game reads your GPS and spawns ghosts on the actual streets around you. Everyone in the same spot sees the same ghosts.

2

Walk to one

Get inside your glowing 75-meter ring. Rare ghosts drift farther out, so a real walk pays off.

3

Raise your camera

Tap the ghost and your camera opens. The ghost floats over the real world in front of you, dodging as you aim.

4

Blast it

Hold the BLAST button to fire your lightning stream. Keep it on target, watch your heat gauge, and drain the ghost before it slips away.

In your camera

Inside 75 meters the hunt goes to augmented reality: the ghost floats over your live camera view, dodging while you hold the lightning stream on it. Simulated previews; in play, the background is whatever your camera sees.

The cast

Eight original spooks, from pushovers to a boss that wants your whole crew. Myrtle only drifts out at breakfast time, 6 to 11 am.

Hunt as a crew

Set a shared crew name with your friends. Every crewmate active in the last ten minutes stacks +0.5× onto your lightning stream, up to double power. That is how you melt Gloombert. A free shared ledger keeps one leaderboard for everyone.
